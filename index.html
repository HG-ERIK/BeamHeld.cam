Privacy Policy - BeamHeld.cam

Last updated: 20 September 2026

BeamHeld.cam ("the app") turns an Android phone into a virtual camera for the PC game BeamNG.drive. This policy explains what the app does with your data. It is written to describe the app's actual behaviour.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own.
  • Camera images never leave your phone and are never recorded.
  • Motion data is sent only to the PC address you type in yourself, over your own network.
  • The developer receives no personal data from you at all.
  • Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, which does collect data - see below.

Camera

The app uses the camera through Google ARCore to work out how the phone is moving in space. The camera preview is shown on screen and processed on the device in real time. The app does not record, save, upload or transmit any photos or video, and no image data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Motion sensors

The app reads the phone's rotation sensors to track orientation. This data is used only to move the in-game camera and is not stored.

Data sent over your network

While the camera screen is open, the app sends the phone's orientation and position to the IP address and port you enter in Settings, using UDP on your local network. This traffic goes only to the computer you specify. It does not pass through any server belonging to the developer, and it contains no personal information - only camera orientation and position numbers.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally, which is removed when you uninstall the app:

  • The PC IP address and port you entered
  • Whether you have purchased "Remove ads"

This information stays on your phone and is not transmitted anywhere.

Advertising

The free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may collect and process device information including the Android advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. The developer does not receive this information and cannot identify individual users from it.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy & Terms. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings > Privacy > Ads on your phone.

Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. Buying "Remove ads" stops all advertising in the app.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Payment details are processed by Google and are never seen by or sent to the developer. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned.

Analytics

The app contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
